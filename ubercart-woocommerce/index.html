Why Migrate

Why Migrate from Ubercart to WooCommerce?

Ubercart runs on Drupal — a more feature-rich fork of the original open-source eCommerce platforms of the era. It is free, functional, and has powered thousands of successful stores. But the platform is now showing its age in every measurable way: a dated admin, fewer than 2,000 plugins, PHP compatibility challenges on modern servers, and a developer pool that has shifted almost entirely to WooCommerce.

  • 💰

    Dramatically Lower Total Cost

    Drupal + Ubercart development is specialised and expensive. WordPress + WooCommerce developers are far more abundant and affordable — and with 60,000+ plugins covering most use cases, you need fewer custom builds from day one.

  • 🔧

    Simpler for Your Team to Manage

    WooCommerce runs on WordPress — the world's most popular CMS. Your marketing team can update products, content, and promotions without a developer.

  • 🚀

    Faster Performance Out of the Box

    WooCommerce on managed hosting (WP Engine, Kinsta, Cloudways) delivers sub-2-second load times and passes Core Web Vitals — giving your store measurable SEO and conversion improvements over Drupal.

  • 🔌

    Massive Plugin Ecosystem

    60,000+ WordPress plugins including Klaviyo, ReCharge, Yotpo, and every payment gateway you need — most available free or at low monthly cost.
