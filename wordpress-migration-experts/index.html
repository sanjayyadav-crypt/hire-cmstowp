About Our Service

WordPress Migration Services: What Makes CmsToWP Different?

CmsToWP is a specialist WordPress migration agency with over 15 years of experience moving websites from every major CMS and eCommerce platform to WordPress and WooCommerce. We've completed 6,200+ migrations — from small business blogs on Wix to enterprise eCommerce stores on Magento with tens of thousands of products. Every migration follows the same rigorous process: zero downtime, 100% data integrity, and full SEO preservation from day one.

The technical challenge of migration isn't moving content — it's preserving your Google rankings while doing it. Every platform uses different URL structures, and without expert 301 redirect mapping, a migration can wipe out years of built-up SEO equity in days. Our migration specialists configure the full redirect structure before go-live, rebuild all meta data in WordPress, and run a post-launch SEO audit on every project.

Who Uses CmsToWP Migration Services?

Our clients include businesses escaping Drupal's complexity and cost, Joomla store owners whose developer pool is shrinking, Wix and Squarespace users who want to own their platform, and eCommerce brands moving off Magento, Shopify, or BigCommerce to WooCommerce to eliminate monthly platform fees and transaction charges. The common thread: they want a clean migration, no ranking loss, and a WordPress site that their team can actually manage.
